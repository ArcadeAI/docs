# Set up strong authentication

Attackers often attempt to steal and reuse passwords. To keep your administrator account safe, Arcade Cloud requires strong authentication to sign in. If Arcade refused your sign-in in the dashboard, the Arcade CLI, or an MCP client, this page shows you how to fix it.

<Callout type="info">
  This page is for anyone who signs in to **Arcade Cloud** with an Arcade.dev
  account. If you sign in through your company's single sign-on (SSO), or your
  company runs its own Arcade deployment, your SSO provider manages the
  strong authentication policy for you.
</Callout>

Strong authentication means signing in with a passkey, or adding a second factor (an authenticator app or a security key) to your password or social sign-in. It takes a minute or two to set up in **Manage my account**.

## Choose a method

| Method                          | How you sign in                                                                         |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Passkey** (recommended)       | Your face, your fingerprint, your device PIN, or your password manager. Nothing to type. |
| **Authenticator app**           | Your password or social sign-in, then a 6-digit code from 1Password, Google Authenticator, or a similar app. |
| **Security key**                | Your password or social sign-in, then a tap on a hardware key like a YubiKey.           |

A password or a social sign-in on its own doesn't count.

A passkey is the strongest choice: nobody can phish it or reuse it, and it counts as strong authentication on its own.

## Set up your method in Manage my account

Open **Manage my account** in either of these ways:

- Open your user menu in the [Arcade dashboard](https://app.arcade.dev/dashboard) and select **Manage my account**.
- If the dashboard shows **Arcade requires secure sign-in**, select **Manage my account** in that dialog.

Then follow the steps for the method you chose.

### Add a passkey

1. Find **Passkey** under **Sign-in methods** and select **Add passkey**.
2. Select **Create a passkey**, then follow your browser's prompt. You can use your face, your fingerprint, your device PIN, or your phone.

Add a passkey on each device you use, so losing one device doesn't lock you out.

### Set up an authenticator app

1. Find **Authenticator app** under **Second factor** and select **Set up**.
2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app. If your app can't scan, copy the setup key shown with the QR code into the app instead.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from your app, then select **Verify**.

### Add a security key

1. Find **Security key** under **Second factor** and select **Add key**.
2. Enter a name in **Name this key** so you can tell your keys apart later, then select **Add security key**.
3. Insert or tap your security key when your browser asks.

### Reconnect with stronger authentication

When you finish, Manage my account shows **Your account is protected**. Then sign in again:

- **Arcade dashboard:** sign in again.
- **Arcade CLI:** run `arcade login` again.
- **MCP client:** sign in to Arcade in your system browser, then reconnect to the Arcade MCP URL. Each client does this differently, so check your client's guide under [MCP clients](/get-started/mcp-clients).

## Having trouble?

### You set up a method, but Arcade still refuses you

Your browser may still hold an Arcade Cloud session from before you set up your passkey or second factor. Sign out of Arcade in this browser by selecting **Sign out** in the Arcade Dashboard, and then try again.

### The passkey prompt closed or timed out

Arcade didn't save anything. Select **Add passkey** again, and finish the browser's prompt before it times out. If your browser or device can't create passkeys, set up an authenticator app or a security key instead.

### The authenticator code doesn't match

Codes change every 30 seconds, so enter the code your app shows right now. If codes keep failing, make sure your phone sets its time automatically.

### You're locked out

If you can't sign in with any of your methods, <ContactEmail domain="arcade.dev" user="support">email Arcade support</ContactEmail> to recover your account.
