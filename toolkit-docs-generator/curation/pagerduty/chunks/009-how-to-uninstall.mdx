## How to uninstall

Remove the integration in both Arcade and PagerDuty. Revoking a connection in Arcade deletes the token Arcade stores, but it doesn't revoke the authorization in PagerDuty.

### In Arcade

- **Remove the tools from your gateway.** In **MCP gateways**, open the gateway's actions menu and select **Edit gateway**. Under **Select apps**, clear **PagerDuty**, then click **Save changes**. To remove the whole gateway, select **Delete gateway** instead.
- **Delete stored authorizations.** Go to **Connections** > **Connected users**, filter the list to the PagerDuty provider, and click **Revoke** on each connection. Revoking requires the Admin or Member project role.

### In PagerDuty

Go to **Integrations** > **OAuth Apps** and select **Arcade.dev**. Then:

- **To revoke your own access**, click **Revoke Your User Token**.
- **To revoke every user's access** (administrators only), click **Revoke All User Tokens**. Each user must authorize again to keep using the tools.
- **To remove the app from your account** (administrators only), click **Uninstall**. This revokes every user's token, and nobody can authorize the app again until an administrator reinstalls it.
