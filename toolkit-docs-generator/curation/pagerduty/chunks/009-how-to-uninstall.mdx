## How to uninstall

Revoking in Arcade deletes the stored token but leaves the authorization in PagerDuty, so do both:

- **In Arcade:** edit the gateway and clear **PagerDuty** under **Select apps**, or delete the gateway. Then revoke the PagerDuty connections under **Connections** > **Connected users**.
- **In PagerDuty:** go to **Integrations** > **OAuth Apps** > **Arcade.dev** and click **Revoke Your User Token**. Administrators can click **Revoke All User Tokens** instead, or **Uninstall** to remove the app from the account.
