## Requirements

- **A PagerDuty account with Arcade's PagerDuty app installed.** A PagerDuty administrator installs the app once for the whole account. See the [integration walkthrough](#integration-walkthrough).
- **An Arcade account that's a member of your Arcade project.** <SignupLink linkLocation="docs:pagerduty-integration-guide">Create an Arcade account</SignupLink> if you don't have one.
- **An MCP client**, such as Claude, Cursor, or VS Code. You can also try the tools in the Arcade dashboard's Playground without one.

Each user authorizes with their own PagerDuty account, so the tools can only do what that user's PagerDuty role allows.

If you self-host the Arcade Engine, or you're building an agent for people who aren't members of your Arcade project, register your own Scoped OAuth app instead. See the [PagerDuty auth provider](/references/auth-providers/pagerduty).
