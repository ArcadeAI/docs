## Requirements

- A PagerDuty account where an administrator has installed Arcade's PagerDuty app. See the [walkthrough](#integration-walkthrough).
- An Arcade account in your Arcade project. <SignupLink linkLocation="docs:pagerduty-integration-guide">Create one</SignupLink> if you don't have one.
- An MCP client such as Claude, Cursor, or VS Code, or the Playground in the Arcade dashboard.

If you self-host Arcade, or you're building an agent for people outside your Arcade project, register your own Scoped OAuth app with the [PagerDuty auth provider](/references/auth-providers/pagerduty).
