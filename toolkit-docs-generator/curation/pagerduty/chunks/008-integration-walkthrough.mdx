## Integration walkthrough

### In PagerDuty

A PagerDuty administrator installs Arcade's app once per account:

1. Sign in as a user with the Admin or Account Owner role and go to `https://<your-subdomain>.pagerduty.com/oauth-apps/PHRMXL1`.
2. Review the requested scopes and click **Install on your account**.

If you aren't an administrator, click **Copy install link** on that page and send the link to one. You don't need a service integration or an integration key.

### In Arcade

1. In the [Arcade dashboard](https://app.arcade.dev), go to **MCP gateways** and click **Create MCP gateway**. Under **Select apps**, select **PagerDuty**. Under **Choose who connects**, select **Members of this Project**. Then create the gateway. For the other options, see [Create via Dashboard](/operate/governance/mcp-gateways/create-via-dashboard).
2. Open the gateway's actions menu, select **Connect to client**, and follow the steps for your MCP client.
3. Ask your agent a PagerDuty question, such as "Which incidents are open right now?" Open the authorization link it returns, sign in to PagerDuty, and click **Submit Consent**.

To try the tools without an MCP client, open the Playground in the Arcade dashboard, click **Select Tools**, and add PagerDuty.
